# ZauberCMS Licensing Model

ZauberCMS uses a source-available model.

## Community / non-commercial use

The source may be used for personal, educational, evaluation and other genuinely non-commercial purposes according to `LICENSE.md`.

## Commercial use

A commercial license is required for business operation, customer projects, paid services or other commercial deployments.

Planned commercial tiers may include:

- Pro — one commercial website
- Business — multiple owned websites
- Agency — customer/client deployments
- Enterprise — negotiated rights, support and deployment terms

Exact pricing and entitlements will be defined before the first commercial release.

## Sponsorship

Sponsorship supports development but does not automatically grant commercial usage rights unless a sponsorship tier explicitly states otherwise.

## Important

This document describes the intended product model. The legally binding permissions are defined by `LICENSE.md` and any separate commercial license agreement issued by Multimedia Zauber.
