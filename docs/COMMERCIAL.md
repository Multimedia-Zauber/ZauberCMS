# Commercial Edition Planning

ZauberCMS is planned as a source-available CMS with optional commercial licenses.

Potential commercial capabilities include:

- commercial deployment rights;
- Pro modules;
- license-key based update access;
- priority support;
- white-label options;
- agency/customer deployment rights;
- enterprise agreements.

Pricing and exact entitlements are intentionally not fixed during pre-alpha development and will be finalized before commercial release.
