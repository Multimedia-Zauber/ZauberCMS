# Code of Conduct

ZauberCMS aims to maintain a professional, constructive and welcoming project environment.

Contributors and participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus on technical issues;
- give constructive feedback;
- avoid harassment, discrimination and personal attacks;
- respect privacy and do not publish private information without permission;
- report security vulnerabilities privately according to `SECURITY.md`.

Project maintainers may moderate or remove contributions and participation that materially disrupt the project or violate these expectations.

For conduct-related concerns, contact Multimedia Zauber privately.
